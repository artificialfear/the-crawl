How the show works
Quest XP = threat base (Trash mob 10, Grunt 20, Elite 40, Mini-boss 80, Raid boss 150) + one-third of your time estimate in minutes (estimates cap at 240). Gold is a quarter of the XP.
Accuracy bonus: +15% when the time you enter lands within 20% under or 25% over your estimate. One-click claims at your estimate are quicker but earn no bonus and don't count toward your accuracy history.
Streak: +5% for each consecutive day you complete a quest, up to +25%.
Damage: every morning each overdue quest hits you for 2× its threat (Trash mob 4 up to Raid boss 24 HP), growing 25% for each extra day overdue (up to 2.5×). Armor and other damage cuts count fully up to 30%, then at half value, capped at 60%. Hit 0 HP and you're knocked out: you drop a fifth of your gold and lose your streak.
Floors: each week is a new floor with a ladder of six bosses: Neighborhood, Borough, City, Province, Country and Floor. Every XP you earn hits the boss in front of you; kill it and the next one steps up, with extra damage carrying over. Drops: Bronze, Bronze, Silver, Silver, Gold, Legendary. Climb as high as you can before the stairwell closes Sunday night. Overdue quests wound you and heal the current boss. At 0 HP you're knocked out: streak lost, a fifth of your gold dropped.
Stats level up from the quests you assign them. Each level above 1 adds: Strength +2% boss damage (max 30%), Intelligence +1% XP (max 15%), Constitution +5 max HP (max 75), Dexterity a 2% wider accuracy window on each side (max 20%), Charisma +2% gold and more viewers (max 30%).
Loot boxes come from achievements, level-ups (Bronze, or Silver every fifth level), bosses and the shop. Contents are random: gold, potions, or gear from Common to Legendary. Duplicates turn into gold.
Gear: weapons, helmets and capes can raise XP; gloves add boss damage; body armor reduces overdue damage. Your best item in each slot counts, whatever your crawler wears. Rings each have their own perk, and only the ring you wear counts. Pet trinkets boost your pet's own passive while it wears one. Unique items are very rare, glow, and carry an extra effect.
Raid boss cap: only two Raid boss quests open at once.
Repeating quests pay full XP each time, then rest until their next turn. Steps pay up to 60% of a quest's base XP as you check them off.
Pets: pick one of seven starters; three more hatch from Mystery Eggs (8% of Gold Boxes, 20% of Legendary Boxes). Your active pet gives a passive bonus that grows with bond level (1–10) plus a special ability. Every quest you finish builds bond, faster with Charisma. Go three days without finishing a quest and your pet sulks: its bonuses pause until you finish one.
Recap episodes: every boss kill gets a short recap and every floor gets a full finale, whether you clear it or the stairwell closes on you. They wait for you in the banner above the boss and the Episode guide, which keeps the last 10.
Consumables: Potions of Focus (double XP on your next quest), Scrolls of Extension (push a due date back 2 days) and Smoke Bombs (skip one occurrence of a repeating quest, chain intact). They drop from boxes and the shop sells them. Scrolls and bombs are used from a quest's ⋯ menu.
Escaping bosses: if the stairwell closes with bosses still standing, you lose 5% of your gold per boss left (up to 30%) and 10% of your viewers, and you start the next floor as a Straggler (−10% XP for its first two days). Your first floor is exempt.
Floor themes: every floor (one week) has a theme with a boon and a bane, like half-price shopping that pays less gold, or hidden boss HP with extra damage. Themes never repeat back to back, and the finale episode teases the next one.
Race & class: when Floor 3 opens you pick a race (shifts your stat levels and adds a quirk) and a class (a perk that rewards how you work). You're offered 3 of each, tilted toward your history, with one reroll per step. It's permanent.
Safe room: your first stop each day. Rest once for HP, take one bounty that must be finished today, and read your fan mail. Once per floor you can shelter for a day: the next morning's overdue damage (and boss healing) is blocked, but your quests earn no viewers that day.
Viewers go up when you get things done and down when quests rot. Each viewer milestone brings a sponsor offer: a box now, plus a 3-day contract that pays a gold bonus if you finish it or costs you gold if you miss it.
Crawler rankings: you versus invented rival crawlers, scored by boss damage this floor. Their pace scales with yours. The top 10 (from day 3) and #1 (from midweek) each pay out once per floor.
